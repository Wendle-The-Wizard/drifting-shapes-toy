# Drifting Shapes Toy

A quiet little toy where soft glowing shapes drift across the screen and gently follow your pointer.

Right now (step 3 of 5) the pale circles, triangles and squares float slowly on their own, turning a little as they go. When one drifts off an edge it comes back in from the opposite side.

To try it, open the page in a browser, on a computer or a phone. Just watch for now. Following the pointer and soft trails come next!
